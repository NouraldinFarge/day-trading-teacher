# Learner glossary v7

## Ask
Lowest displayed price at which a seller is offering shares.

## Bid
Highest displayed price at which a buyer is offering shares.

## Binding limit
The rule that produces the smallest permitted quantity or exposure.

## Buying power
Broker-calculated capacity to purchase or short securities; it does not replace a personal loss boundary.

## Correlation
A measure of how variables or positions tend to move together; it does not prove causation.

## Decision point
The fixed time at which the learner must decide using only then-available information.

## Execution allowance
An extra per-share amount used in planned-risk modeling for possible friction.

## Expectancy
Average result per observation under a defined method and dataset.

## Freeriding
Buying and selling a security before paying for the purchase, subject to current broker and regulatory treatment.

## Good-faith violation
A cash-account issue that may arise when a purchase funded with unsettled proceeds is sold before the funds used for it settle.

## Halt
A temporary pause in trading imposed by an exchange, regulator, or market mechanism.

## Holdout
Data reserved from strategy development and used only after the method is frozen.

## House requirement
A broker requirement that can be stricter than a regulatory minimum.

## Intraday margin deficit
The amount by which required intraday margin exceeds available margin under the firm’s current method.

## Invalidation
The objective condition showing the original trade idea no longer applies.

## Locate
A broker-dealer Regulation SHO obligation to have reasonable grounds to believe shares can be borrowed before effecting certain short sales.

## Marketable limit
A limit order priced so it can trade immediately against available opposite-side quotes, while retaining a price boundary.

## Median
The middle ordered value, or the average of the two middle values when the count is even.

## Not scorable
A result used when required evidence does not exist or is late. NS-NA means the dimension is outside the case blueprint.

## Overfitting
Adapting a method too closely to past data so it may not transfer.

## Partial fill
Execution of only part of an order quantity.

## Quote age
Elapsed time since the quote source timestamp.

## R-multiple
A result expressed relative to the planned initial risk amount.

## Rule 201
A Regulation SHO price-test restriction that can limit prices at which short sales may be effected after a qualifying decline.

## Settled cash
Cash shown by the broker as settled and available under current account controls.

## Spread
Difference between displayed ask and bid.

## Time in force
An instruction governing how long an order remains active, such as Day.

## Uncertainty interval
A range used to express uncertainty around an estimate; its meaning depends on the method.

## Unsettled proceeds
Sale proceeds that have not yet reached the settlement date.

## Secure form
A neutral key-free assessment packet released individually by a facilitator.

## Outcome-hidden
A condition in which the learner is not told the intended response before locking the artifact.

## Parallel form
A new case designed to measure the same construct and difficulty without reusing an opened answer.

## Packet fingerprint
A SHA-256 value used to confirm exactly which learner packet was released and scored.
