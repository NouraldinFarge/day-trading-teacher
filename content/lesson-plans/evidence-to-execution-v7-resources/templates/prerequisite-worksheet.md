# Prerequisite worksheet

1. Label each number with its unit: dollars, shares, dollars per share, percent, or seconds.
2. For five long and short examples, compute technical risk per share.
3. Add the stated execution allowance.
4. Divide maximum planned loss by planned risk per share and round down.
5. Read the eight ticket fields from a static ticket image or text alternative.
6. Explain why completing this worksheet does not establish live-trading readiness.
