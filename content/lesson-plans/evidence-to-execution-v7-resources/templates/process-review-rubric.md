# Process review rubric v7

## Evidence

- **0:** Claims lack a source or timestamp, contradict the supplied record, or use reveal information as if it were known earlier.
- **1:** Some relevant facts are captured, but chronology, source labels, calculations, conflicts, or unknowns are incomplete.
- **2:** All required sources, timestamps, calculations, chronology, conflicts, and unknowns are complete before reveal.
- **3:** Level 2 plus the learner resolves a source conflict using the stated hierarchy and writes a precise future capture rule.

## Account And Readiness

- **0:** A required account, session, data, halt, broker, or platform gate is skipped or a blocking condition is ignored.
- **1:** Most gates are checked, but one material condition is assumed, stale, unsupported, or left unresolved.
- **2:** Every applicable gate is checked with dated evidence; missing or conflicting evidence produces wait or stop.
- **3:** Level 2 plus a secondary control is checked and the verification source, effective date, or status timestamp is documented.

## Playbook And Eligibility

- **0:** No frozen setup or objective eligibility rule is applied.
- **1:** A setup is named, but one threshold lacks units, source, baseline, calibration, or a missing-data response.
- **2:** The frozen setup and all simultaneous calibrated gates are applied exactly as written.
- **3:** Level 2 plus threshold provenance and interactions are explained without changing the rule after seeing the outcome.

## Risk

- **0:** Risk inputs are absent, mathematically wrong, or exceed a preset boundary.
- **1:** Core arithmetic is partly correct, but allowance, rounding, combined exposure, binding limit, or zero quantity is mishandled.
- **2:** Maximum loss, invalidation distance, allowance, whole-share size, and binding limit are correct and rounded down.
- **3:** Level 2 plus an alternate fill, partial fill, correlation, or remaining-session scenario is stress-tested correctly in the same artifact.

## Plan

- **0:** The plan is written after reveal or lacks a usable trigger and invalidation.
- **1:** A pre-reveal plan exists, but one or more required fields, contingencies, or cancellation rules are vague or missing.
- **2:** The basic card and all applicable advanced fields are complete before the ticket or reveal.
- **3:** Level 2 plus a changed-premise contingency is explicit and the learner cancels or rebuilds when that premise changes.

## Execution

- **0:** The order response is unsupported, duplicates possible exposure, or ignores a known failure mode.
- **1:** An order label is named without explaining price, non-fill, trigger, gap, priority, or partial-fill risk.
- **2:** The selected order or no-order response matches the failure the plan can accept.
- **3:** Level 2 plus partial fills, gaps, halts, Rule 201, or reconnect state are handled without duplicate or uncontrolled exposure.

## Behavior And Review

- **0:** Outcome changes the score, the original is edited after reveal, or the learner continues after a preset stop.
- **1:** The review is vague, self-critical, or not linked to evidence and a bounded correction.
- **2:** The original is preserved, outcome and process are separated, scores cite artifacts, and one neutral correction is specified.
- **3:** Level 2 plus the correction includes a measurable test condition, assigned future case ID, and stop rule.

## Not-scorable codes

- **NS-MISSING:** Required evidence does not exist.
- **NS-LATE:** The evidence was created after reveal or after the decision point.
- **NS-CONFLICT:** A material source conflict is unresolved.
- **NS-NA:** The dimension is not part of this case blueprint and must not be included in the case pass calculation.
