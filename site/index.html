One connected learning system

Tools serve the lesson, and evidence returns to it.

Every feasible lesson can open a Decision Card, chart replay, Journal, or Learning Lab mission. The resulting artifacts remain connected in one learning case instead of becoming isolated app activity.

Structured curriculum

Brief first. Attempt before answer. Transfer before mastery.

Thirteen core lessons, imported lesson plans, objective checks, fresh-case remediation, separated practice dates, and explicit evidence standards keep completion distinct from mastery.

Guided lesson entry

Know the purpose before doing the work.

Every lesson begins with a concise guide, makes its evidence standard visible, and keeps resulting plans, replays, paper sessions, reflections, and drills linked to one learning case.

Evidence Journal

Understand behavior without inventing intent.

Calendar, heatmap, equity and drawdown views, breakdowns, process goals, reviewed AI-draft handoffs, and newest-to-oldest trade lessons preserve uncertainty and source evidence.

Calendar and process heatmap

See consistency without creating a trade quota.

Monthly and yearly views can switch among P&L, activity, journal completion, and discipline. Rest days and no-trade days never erase process history.

Healthy progression

Reward discipline, correction, and reflection.

Rest-day-safe activity, tiered achievements, exact evidence ledgers, sample gates, and process XP avoid rewarding trade frequency, position size, or screen time.

Responsible by construction

Powerful practice with bright safety boundaries.

The app does
  • Teach deterministic decision and risk processes
  • Replay imported or provider-labeled historical OHLCV
  • Read supported Fidelity exports locally and incrementally
  • Create user-controlled lesson and journal handoff files
  • Protect provider keys with Windows Credential Manager
The app does not
  • Generate live signals or personalized recommendations
  • Authenticate to Fidelity, scrape screens, or place orders
  • Present historical bars as execution-quality quotes
  • Upload records or run a hidden model automatically
  • Diagnose mental state or promise trading outcomes
