# Community expectations

Day-Trading Teacher discussions should be respectful, specific, evidence-driven, and safe for people learning around financially sensitive material.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical, educational, accessibility, and product behavior rather than judging individuals.
- Protect private financial information and use synthetic or fully redacted examples.
- Separate facts, calculations, reported intent, uncertainty, and opinion.
- Challenge claims constructively and link current code, tests, documentation, or authoritative sources when possible.
- Respect the product's educational boundary and avoid personalized investment advice.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing credentials, account identifiers, order histories, journal entries, private correspondence, secure assessments, or other personal information.
- Deceptive performance claims, return promises, promotional spam, or pressure to trade.
- Requests or contributions that add live signals, brokerage authentication, account scraping, screen watching, hidden AI calls, or order execution.
- Deliberately disrupting issue, review, security, or release processes.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, and restrict participation when these expectations are violated. Public technical and documentation concerns belong in the appropriate issue template; vulnerabilities or reports involving exposed private data belong in [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/NouraldinFarge/day-trading-teacher/security/advisories/new).
